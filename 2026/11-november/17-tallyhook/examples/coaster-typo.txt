Flat coaster (US terms)
Rnd 1: 8 sc in magic ring, join with sl st to first sc (8)
Rnd 2: ch 1, 2 sc in each st around, join (16)
Rnd 3: ch 1, *sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around, join (24)
Rnd 4: ch 1, *sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around, join (30)
Rnd 5: ch 1, *sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around, join (40)
Rnd 6: ch 1, *sc in next 4 sts, inc; rep from * around, join (54)
