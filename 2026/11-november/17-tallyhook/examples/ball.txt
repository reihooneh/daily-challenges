Amigurumi ball (US terms), 4 mm hook, worsted yarn

Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: *sc in next st, inc; rep from * around (18)
Rnd 4: (2 sc, inc) x 6 (24)
Rnd 5: [sc in next 3 sts, inc] 6 times (30)
Rnd 6: *sc 4, inc* around (36)
Rnds 7-12: sc in each st around (36)
Rnd 13: (sc in next 4 sts, dec) x 6 (30)
Rnd 14: (3 sc, dec) x 6 (24)
Rnd 15: (2 sc, dec) x 6 (18)
