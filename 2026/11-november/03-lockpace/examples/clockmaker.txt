# An invented room, as a designer might sketch it before the build.
room The Clockmaker's Study
limit 60
players 2-6
show 4
goal freedom

puzzle Bookshelf cipher (9 min) gives drawer code
puzzle Desk drawer (3 min) needs drawer code gives brass key, small key
puzzle Portrait eyes (7 min) gives star chart
puzzle Telescope (8 min, 2 people) needs star chart gives lens
puzzle Map drawer (6 min) gives map piece
puzzle Globe (7 min) needs map piece gives compass
puzzle Clock face (10 min) needs brass key gives gear
puzzle Music box (6 min) needs small key gives melody
puzzle Pipe organ (7 min) needs melody gives organ note
puzzle Pendulum cabinet (9 min) needs gear, lens, compass gives pendulum
puzzle Floor tiles (8 min) gives tile number, old coin
puzzle Wall safe (5 min) needs tile number, organ note gives safe key
puzzle Grandfather clock (5 min, 3 people) needs pendulum uses safe key gives exit key
puzzle Exit door (1 min) uses exit key gives freedom
