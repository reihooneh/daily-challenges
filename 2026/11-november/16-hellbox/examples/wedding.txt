title: Invitation
Together with their families
Wilhelmina Quill & Owen Dufresne
request the pleasure of your company
at their wedding
on Saturday the twenty-first of November
at four o'clock in the afternoon
Woodbridge Mill, Bowral
---
title: Reply card
Kindly reply by the first of October
Name
will joyfully attend
will be with you in spirit
---
title: Menu
Wood-fired flatbread with whipped feta
Slow-roasted lamb, figs & walnuts
Buffalo mozzarella, peach & mint
Raspberry & pistachio pavlova
---
title: Order of the day
Four o'clock: the ceremony in the willow grove
Five o'clock: drinks by the millpond
Seven o'clock: dinner & dancing
