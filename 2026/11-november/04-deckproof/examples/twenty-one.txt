# The classic 21-card trick: deal three piles, the spectator points to the pile with their card,
# gather it in the middle, three times. The card is then the 11th.
trick The twenty-one card trick
deck 21
choose card X            # the spectator thinks of any card
repeat 3
  piles 3                # deal into three piles, face down
  pick X                 # "which pile is your card in?"
  gather middle          # that pile goes between the other two
end
expect X at 11
