# A cherry hall table with breadboard ends and a walnut panel in the back.
# Axes: x runs left to right, y front to back, z up. Lengths are in millimetres.
design Hall table
climate 30 to 65          # indoor relative humidity over a year, %
built-at 45               # humidity in the shop when it was made
temperature 20

part top          cherry   flat     grain x  width y
part end-left     cherry   flat     grain y  width x     # breadboard ends across the top
part end-right    cherry   flat     grain y  width x
part apron-front  cherry   flat     grain x  width z
part apron-side   cherry   flat     grain y  width z
part leg          cherry   quarter  grain z  width x
part back-rail    cherry   flat     grain x  width z
part back-panel   walnut   flat     grain z  width x

joint top end-left         glue         along y 450              # glued all along: the classic mistake
joint top end-right        pegs         along y 450
joint top apron-front      glue         along x 1100             # long grain to long grain
joint top apron-side       buttons 2    along y 400
joint apron-front leg      glue         along z 90               # a wide tenon
joint back-panel back-rail floating 6 depth 10 along x 400
